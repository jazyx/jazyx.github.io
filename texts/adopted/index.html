Adopted

This country is white. It is so white and so cold and so beautiful. But the house is warm. The house is nice. There is food here.

The little girl is standing.

This is not her country. Her country, her city, her home is far away. This nice man and that white woman are not her father and mother, not her grandfather and grandmother. Her home is so far away.

This cold white country is so wide.

This room is big and clean. One small bed, a table, a chair, a painting, a toy. A soft toy. A bunny. And also a teddy bear. The floor is golden. Yellow gold. The floor is warm wood. The bed is soft and warm.

She is standing.

She wants to cry, she wants to eat, she wants... something. She is really quiet. She wants to be at home. She wants her own bed. The right bed, her messy bed.

This is not her father. It is not her mother. They are old and white. Are they really nice?

There is food here.

"Пожалуйста," she says quietly.

"Ole hyvä," says the old man who is not her father.

"Olehyva," the young girl says. The word is strange. It is like the language of angels singing.

She is still standing.

The room is big. The bed is soft and comfy. Just one bed. She wants to sit down.

She sits down.

It is over. It is her bed. She is their child now.
